### What is Box Out for Hunger?

Box Out for Hunger is a nonprofit organization that we created to help fight food insecurity in our local community. Our goal is to collect nonperishable food items and donate them to people and families who need extra support. We believe everyone deserves access to food and that no one should have to worry about where their next meal will come from.
Through food drives and partnerships with local sports organizations, we hope to make a positive difference in our community. By collecting donations and bringing people together, Box Out for Hunger works to raise awareness about food insecurity and provide support to those who need it most.

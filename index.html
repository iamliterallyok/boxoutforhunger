01Mission & Community

What Is Box Out For Hunger?

Box Out for Hunger is a nonprofit organization created to help fight food insecurity in our local community. Our goal is to collect nonperishable food items and donate them to people and families who need extra support. We believe everyone deserves access to food and that no one should have to worry about where their next meal will come from.

02Where It Started

Why We Started

We started Box Out for Hunger because we wanted to find a way to give back to our community through something we love: basketball. We realized that basketball could be more than just a sport. It could be a way to bring people together and help make a difference in the lives of others.

Our mission is to use our love of basketball to help families facing food insecurity. By going to local food drives and working with local sports organizations to collect nonperishable goods, we hope to encourage others to give back and support people in need. We want Box Out for Hunger to show that even small acts of kindness can have a big impact on a community and that your age doesn’t define how much you can give back to your community.
